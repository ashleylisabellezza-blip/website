About O&M

O&M, short for Original & Mineral, was founded by Sydney salon owner Jose Bryce Smith. Alongside its hair care and styling products, the brand makes professional hair color.

O&M says its hair care is made in its own solar-powered factory in Sydney, Australia, that its products are PETA-approved, and that it packages them using recycled aluminium and post-consumer recycled (PCR) materials.
