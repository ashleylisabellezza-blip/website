Experienced Hair Stylist

If you have a love for the hair and beauty industry and have experience behind the chair with a strong foundation of skills… we would love to meet you.

We have positions available for stylists that have been working behind the chair and can execute all haircut and color techniques.

Formula for success includes

  • Solid hair fundamentals
  • Passion for education and the continual building of your skills, both technique and mind
  • The ability to lead and educate young stylists on your team
  • Positive attitude
  • Problem solver
  • Team player

Team role

  • Provide outstanding customer service
  • Meeting and exceeding service, retention, and retail goals
  • Attend and participate in education
  • Accommodate a growing clientele
  • Support your team and assist in any salon duties needed

This position requires a license from the Ohio State Cosmetology and Barber Board.
