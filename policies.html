Policies & guest information

Thank you for choosing Bellezza & Co. These policies protect your time, our providers’ schedules and the quiet of the salon and spa. Please read them before your visit.

Changes & cancellations

Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice to change, reschedule or cancel an appointment. That gives us the chance to offer the time to another guest, and it respects your provider’s time.

To change an appointment, call us at 740-366-1604.

No-shows & same-day cancellations

A no-show or same-day cancellation means cancelling or rescheduling within 24 hours of your appointment. It may mean you lose any deposit on that appointment.

Here is how we handle repeat no-shows and same-day cancellations:

  • First time: a friendly reminder of our policy is noted in your account.
  • Second time: a 50% deposit is required to schedule future services.
  • Third time: full prepayment (100%) is required to book future appointments.

Salon & spa etiquette

A few requests so every guest can relax:

  • Please turn off or silence your phone’s ringer.
  • Please don’t use speakerphone in the salon or spa.
  • Please speak quietly in shared spaces.
  • We love children and welcome them for their own scheduled appointments. When you’re here for your own services, please arrange childcare so every guest can enjoy a relaxing visit.

Deposits & prepayment

After repeat no-shows or same-day cancellations, deposit and prepayment requirements stay in place until a consistent appointment history is re-established. Then standard booking may be reinstated, at Bellezza & Co.’s discretion.

Deposits are non-refundable if you give less than 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule.

Keratin smoothing

The GK Keratin Smoothing Treatment needs a consultation and a $50 deposit before we can schedule it. For this service we need 48 hours’ notice of a cancellation, or the deposit is forfeited.

Gift cards

Privacy: website forms

Two pages on this site have forms: the bridal inquiry and the careers application. A form collects what you type into it. When you send it, it goes to our team through our website host’s form service (Netlify).

We keep careers applications and other general messages for 12 months, and bridal inquiries for 24 months, then delete them.

To ask what information we have from a form you sent, or to have it deleted, call us at 740-366-1604 and ask for Lisa Jeffries, our co-owner, who handles these requests.

Website analytics

We count visits to this website with Plausible Analytics, which uses no cookies and collects no personal information. That’s why there’s no cookie banner.

Text messaging & privacy information

Bellezza Spa may use text messaging to communicate with guests regarding appointments, customer service, services, products, and spa updates.

Information we collect

When you subscribe to or communicate with us through our text messaging service, we may collect your mobile phone number, the content of messages sent to and from us, and your opt-in consent information.

How we use your information

We use this information to send text messages related to Bellezza Spa services, products, and updates. We may also use text messaging to communicate with you for customer service purposes, including appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, scheduling updates, and to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Information sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data security

We use reasonable and appropriate security measures to help protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Opting out of text messages

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. After opting out, you will no longer receive text messages from us.

Policy updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes may be communicated by text message, email, or a notice on our website.

2-way SMS communications

These are direct communications to your mobile number from the business. They can confirm status of your appointment, notify you of business closings, and provide responses to messages received.

You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Just text “STOP”. After you send the SMS message “STOP” to us, we will send you an SMS message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us. If you want to join again, reply START to (718) 921-6100.

If you are experiencing issues with the messaging program you can reply with the keyword HELP for more assistance.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

As always, message and data rates may apply for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you. If you have any questions about your text plan or data plan, it is best to contact your wireless provider.
