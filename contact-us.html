Before you visit

How do I book?

Online 24/7 on our online booking page (Meevo), in the Bellezza app for iPhone or Android, or by calling 740-366-1604 during opening hours. How to book

Not sure who to book with?

Call 740-366-1604 and our front desk will match you. Every provider has a photo, title and bio on Meet the Team, and our new guest guide explains how levels and prices work.

What is the cancellation policy?

At least 24 hours’ notice for any appointment change, reschedule or cancellation. After a no-show or same-day cancellation, the first time, a friendly reminder of our policy is noted in your account. The second time, a 50% deposit is required to book future services. The third time, full prepayment (100%) is required to book future appointments. Changes policy

When are you closed for holidays?

Every year we close on New Year’s Day, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day and December 25–26. We close at 12pm on December 24 and 31, and at 5pm on July 3 and on Newark Trick-or-Treat night. Upcoming holiday hours

Can I use a gift card at Slay Aesthetics?

No. Bellezza gift cards are not accepted at Slay Aesthetics. Gift card policy
