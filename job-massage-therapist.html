Massage Therapist

Bellezza & Co. is looking for a Massage Therapist to join our growing salon and spa team. We are open to soon-to-be graduates as well as licensed massage therapists who are looking for a supportive spa home where they can build their career, grow their skills, and care for an established client base.

Our massage department has steady client demand, front desk support, marketing support, and a full salon and spa environment designed to help providers focus on giving excellent guest care. We provide the supplies and products needed for massage therapy and offer continuing education and training opportunities to help you continue growing in your career.

This position can be full-time or part-time. It is a commission-based employee position with a guaranteed hourly base. Gratuities also provide additional earning potential.

Formula for success includes

  • Caring — You have a genuine heart for helping others feel better, relax, and feel cared for.
  • Professional — You understand the importance of boundaries, communication, consistency, and creating a comfortable client experience.
  • Reliable — Your team and your clients can count on you to show up prepared and ready to provide excellent service.
  • Client-focused — You listen to each guest’s needs and customize the experience to help them feel relaxed, supported, and cared for.
  • Eager to grow — You are open to feedback, continuing education, and learning new techniques that help you become a stronger massage therapist.

Team role

  • Provide professional massage therapy services in a relaxing salon and spa environment.
  • Perform relaxation massage, deep tissue massage, and hot stone massage with confidence.
  • Communicate with guests about their comfort, areas of concern, pressure preferences, and service goals.
  • Maintain a clean, calm, and professional treatment room.
  • Follow Bellezza & Co. service standards and client care expectations.
  • Participate in continuing education and training opportunities.
  • Be open to training for additional and specialty massage services.
  • Work with our front desk team to support a smooth client experience.
  • Ability to work evenings and every other Saturday.

Pay and benefits

  • Paid training and assisting program
  • Paid vacation and a 401(k) once eligibility requirements are met
  • Flexible scheduling and planned time off

This position requires a massage therapy license in the state of Ohio. Soon-to-be graduates are welcome to apply.
