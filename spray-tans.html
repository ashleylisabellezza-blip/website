Questions about spray tans

How do I get ready for a spray tan?

Our Before & After Care sheet (PDF) has the manufacturer’s tips. In short: exfoliate 24 hours before, and finish any waxing or shaving 24 hours before. On the day, skip lotions, creams and perfume, and wear loose, dark clothing. Afterwards, avoid water and sweating for at least 4 hours, and wait 16 hours to shower. Questions? Call 740-366-1604.

How much notice do I need to change or cancel?

“We kindly request at least 24 hours’ notice for any appointment changes, reschedules, or cancellations. This allows us the opportunity to accommodate other guests and helps us respect our service providers’ time.” Changes & cancellations

What happens if I miss an appointment or cancel the same day?

A no-show or a cancellation within 24 hours of your appointment may mean you forfeit any deposit on it. If it keeps happening: the first time, we note a friendly reminder in your account; the second time, we ask for a 50% deposit to book future services; the third time, we ask for full prepayment.
