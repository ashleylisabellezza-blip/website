Hair Extensions

Transform your look with our premium weft hair extensions! Luxuriously crafted for seamless blending and natural movement, our extensions offer instant length and volume. Elevate your style effortlessly with our high-quality weft extensions.

Weft hair extensions are a popular type of hair extension that involves attaching hair wefts, which are essentially tracks of hair, onto your natural hair. These extensions are typically applied by sewing them in to beads that have been placed onto the natural hair. Weft extensions come in various lengths, colors, and textures to match your natural hair seamlessly. They provide added volume, length, and versatility to your hairstyle, allowing you to achieve different looks effortlessly. Weft extensions are known for their durability and ability to blend seamlessly with your natural hair for a natural appearance.

Ready to take the next step towards fabulous hair? Book your personalized consultation for hair extensions today! Our expert stylists will guide you through the process, answer all your questions, and help you achieve the look you’ve always wanted. Questions about booking hair

How much notice do I need to change or cancel?

“We kindly request at least 24 hours’ notice for any appointment changes, reschedules, or cancellations. This allows us the opportunity to accommodate other guests and helps us respect our service providers’ time.” Changes & cancellations

Do I need a consultation or deposit for keratin?

Yes. “A consultation and $50 deposit are required prior to scheduling” a GK Keratin Smoothing Treatment. “A 48-hour notice is required for cancellations to avoid forfeiting your deposit.” Deposits

What happens after a no-show or same-day cancellation?

A same-day cancellation means “cancelling or rescheduling within 24 hours of the appointment.” For repeat no-shows or same-day cancellations:

  • First occurrence: A friendly reminder of our policy will be noted in your account.
  • Second occurrence: A 50% deposit will be required to schedule future services.
  • Third occurrence: Full prepayment, 100%, will be required to book future appointments.
