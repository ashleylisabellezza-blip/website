Before and after your wax

Hair needs to be at least ¼ inch long to wax, so please don’t shave between appointments. We recommend waxing every 4 to 6 weeks.

For 24 to 48 hours afterwards, keep the waxed area clean and avoid heat and friction: no hot baths or showers, saunas, hot tubs, tanning or chlorinated pools. Our after-care sheet has the full list. Waxing After Care (PDF)

Booking a facial too? The Nano Needling Dermaplane Treatment is not for skin waxed in the last 5 days, and the Pro Power Peel asks for no waxing in the 72 hours before. If you’re not sure how to space them, call 740-366-1604.

Questions about waxing

Why are there two prices for each wax?

Pricing is based on the level of the Skin Therapist providing the service and is listed as Associate Skin Therapist / Senior Skin Therapist. How our levels work

How much notice do I need to give to change my appointment?

“We kindly request at least 24 hours’ notice for any appointment changes, reschedules, or cancellations. This allows us the opportunity to accommodate other guests and helps us respect our service providers’ time.” Changes & cancellations

What happens if I miss an appointment or cancel the same day?

“A no-show or same-day cancellation, defined as cancelling or rescheduling within 24 hours of the appointment, may result in the forfeiture of any deposit associated with the appointment.” After a first reminder, a second occurrence means a 50% deposit to book future services, and a third means full prepayment.
