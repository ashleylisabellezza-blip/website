2008 · The house

1,800 sq ft

Grew up in a family business

Lisa Jeffries and Ashley Basham were born and raised in Newark. The two sisters, along with their brother, grew up in a family-owned business and always knew they wanted to start one of their own. After cosmetology school they each worked in different salons, until the chance to work together came in 2008.

As the economy was crashing, Lisa and Ashley bought an 1,800 sq ft house to turn into a salon of their own.

The last addition

2022

A new name, Bellezza & Co., and the boutique.

Our last addition was in 2022. We rebranded from Bellezza Salon and Day Spa to Bellezza & Co. and added a boutique to the mix.

In the boutique: Dermalogica, Olaplex, REF, Lakmé, Smashbox, VOESH, Calecim and ECRU New York.

Today

5,500 sq ft

28 people work at Bellezza today.

22 stylists, nail artists, and skin and massage therapists, plus our business manager, our four-person front desk, and Shannon Francis, CNP, of Slay Aesthetics.

Paige, now an Expert Nail Artist, started here as an intern. Lisa and Ashley are both still behind the chair one day a week, and spend the rest of their time growing the business.
