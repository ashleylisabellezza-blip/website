Our story

Grew up in a family business

Lisa Jeffries and Ashley Basham were born and raised in Newark. The two sisters, along with their brother, grew up in a family-owned business and always knew they wanted to start one of their own. After cosmetology school they each worked in different salons, until the chance to work together came in 2008. They opened as Bellezza Salon and Day Spa in 2009 and have been Bellezza & Co. since 2022. Both are still behind the chair one day a week, and spend the rest of their time growing the business.

Timeline

From 2008 to today

  1. 2008

    As the economy was crashing, Lisa and Ashley bought an 1,800 sq ft house to turn into a salon of their own.

  2. 2009

    Bellezza Salon and Day Spa opened.

  3. Since then

    Two additions to the building.

  4. 2016

    Voted the Number 1 spa in Licking County, and every year since.

  5. 2022

    The last addition, a new name, Bellezza & Co., and the boutique.

  6. Today

    5,500 sq ft, 28 people, and both owners still behind the chair one day a week.
