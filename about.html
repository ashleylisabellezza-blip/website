Our story

Grew up in a family business

Lisa Jeffries and Ashley Basham were born and raised in Newark. The two sisters, along with their brother, grew up in a family-owned business and always knew they wanted to start one of their own. After cosmetology school they each worked in different salons, until the chance to work together came in 2008. They opened as Bellezza Salon and Day Spa in 2009 and have been Bellezza & Co. since 2022. After more than two decades behind the chair, Lisa stepped away from regularly providing services in early 2026 to lead the business. Ashley still takes clients while she, too, spends more of her time on the business and its growth.

Timeline

From 2008 to today

  1. 2008

    As the economy was crashing, Lisa and Ashley bought an 1,800 sq ft house to turn into a salon of their own.

  2. 2009

    Bellezza Salon and Day Spa opened.

  3. 2014

    The first addition to the building, in the fall.

  4. 2016

    Voted Best Spa in Licking County in the Licking County Community’s Choice Awards (then Best of Licking County), run by The Newark Advocate, and every year the award has been held since.

  5. 2022

    The last addition, a new name, Bellezza & Co., and the boutique.

  6. 2026

    Voted Best Spa and Best Hair Salon in Licking County. See the 2026 results

  7. Today

    5,500 sq ft and 27 people.

The team today

27 people work at Bellezza today.

20 stylists, nail artists, and skin and massage therapists, plus co-founder Lisa Jeffries, our business manager, our four-person front desk, and Shannon Francis, CNP, of Slay Aesthetics & Wellness.

Bellezza has also been voted Best Hair Salon in Licking County every year the award has been held since 2016, with one runner-up year.

Paige, now an Expert Nail Artist, and Liv, now a Senior Hair Stylist, both started here as interns.
