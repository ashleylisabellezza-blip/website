How pricing works

Most services are priced by your provider’s level. Hair has five levels: Jr Associate, Associate, Senior, Expert and Master. Nails and skin have three: Associate, Senior and Expert.

Levels set the price. Book the level that fits your budget.

Most of our stylists and nail artists have their level in their title on Meet the Team, for example Austyn, Senior Hair Stylist. See the full menus for haircuts and manicures, or all services & prices.

Changes & cancellations

Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice to change, reschedule or cancel. It lets us offer your time to another guest and respects your provider’s schedule.

Cancelling or rescheduling within 24 hours of your appointment counts as a same-day cancellation. After a no-show or a same-day cancellation:

  1. First time: a friendly reminder of our policy is noted in your account.
  2. Second time: a 50% deposit is required to book future services.
  3. Third time: full prepayment (100%) is required to book future appointments.

Deposits are non-refundable without 24 hours’ notice. Deposit and prepayment requirements stay in place until a consistent appointment history is re-established. Read the full policy

Salon etiquette

To keep the salon and spa relaxing for everyone, we ask every guest to:

  • Turn off or silence your phone ringer.
  • Skip speakerphone while you’re in the salon or spa.
  • Speak quietly in shared spaces.
  • Arrange childcare while you’re here for your own services.

New guest questions

How do I choose who to book?

Browse Meet the Team by specialty: every provider has a photo, title and bio. Or take the match quiz, or call 740-366-1604 and our front desk will match you.

Why do prices differ between providers?

Most services are priced by the provider’s level: Jr Associate, Associate, Senior, Expert and Master for hair, and Associate, Senior and Expert for nails and skin. For example, a women’s haircut & finish is $37 with a Jr Associate and $60 with a Master. Levels set the price. Book the level that fits your budget. How pricing works

How do I book?

Online 24/7 on our online booking page (Meevo), in the Bellezza app for iPhone or Android, or by calling 740-366-1604 during opening hours. How to book

How much notice do I need to give to change or cancel?

At least 24 hours’ notice for any appointment change, reschedule or cancellation. Changes policy

What happens after a no-show or same-day cancellation?

The first time, a friendly reminder of our policy is noted in your account. The second time, a 50% deposit is required to book future services. The third time, full prepayment (100%) is required to book future appointments. No-show policy

Can I bring my kids to my appointment?

We ask that childcare arrangements be made while you are here for your own services, so that all guests can enjoy a relaxing visit. Etiquette policy

Do any services need a consultation or a deposit?

The GK Keratin Smoothing Treatment needs a consultation and a $50 deposit before scheduling. Platinum Card Color needs a consultation and a deposit before booking. Fantasy color and specialty braiding are priced at a consultation. Deposits

Can I use a Bellezza gift card at Slay Aesthetics?

No. Bellezza gift cards are not accepted at Slay Aesthetics. Gift card policy
