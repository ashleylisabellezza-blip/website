About Lakmé

Lakmé Hair is a professional salon hair care brand with roots in fashion and runway styling. It works behind the scenes at fashion weeks and with professional stylists, and that work shapes its formulas.

Lakmé Hair products cover a wide range of needs, from repair and hydration to color protection and styling, for the salon and for home.

Order for pickup

  1. Tell us what you would like on our pick-up order form: product, size and quantity.
  2. We send you a payment link by text or email.
  3. Pay, then pick up your order in the salon or curbside at 206 Deo Drive.
