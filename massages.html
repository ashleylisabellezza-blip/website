Questions about massage

Which massage should I choose?

If you want light to medium pressure, book a relaxation massage. If you prefer firm pressure, look at deep tissue or Ashiatsu. Mother-to-be massage is for clients in their 2nd and 3rd trimester. Not sure? Call 740-366-1604 and our front desk will help you pick.

How much notice do I need to change or cancel?

“We kindly request at least 24 hours’ notice for any appointment changes, reschedules, or cancellations. This allows us the opportunity to accommodate other guests and helps us respect our service providers’ time.” Changes & cancellations

What happens if I miss an appointment or cancel the same day?

A no-show or a cancellation within 24 hours of your appointment may mean you forfeit any deposit on it. If it keeps happening: the first time, we note a friendly reminder in your account; the second time, we ask for a 50% deposit to book future services; the third time, we ask for full prepayment. No-shows & same-day cancellations

What should I know about the spa during my visit?

To keep the spa relaxing for everyone, please silence your phone ringer, don’t use speakerphone, and speak quietly in shared spaces. We love children and welcome them for their own appointments, but please arrange childcare while you’re here for yours. Salon & spa etiquette
