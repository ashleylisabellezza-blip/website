Education

You keep learning here.

Our New Talent program includes weekly education classes: theory, mannequin and model work, and business mindset classes. Education is led by Devon, our Salon Manager, Director of Education and Master Stylist.

Bellezza has a long history of working with our local cosmetology schools. Our internship program gives students a chance to see what everyday life looks like in the salon.
