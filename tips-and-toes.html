Questions about nail appointments

Do you remove acrylic?

No. We do not remove acrylic. Manicures do not include gel or enhancement removal unless the service says so. If you are wearing gel or dip, book a service that includes removal, or add Gel or Dip Polish Removal ($20) to your manicure or pedicure. Removal of Gel X is not included in a Gel X Mani and must be booked.

Can children book a manicure or pedicure?

Yes. Tiny Tips and Tiny Toes are for ages 12 and under. Gel polish is not included or recommended for ages 12 and under. We love children and welcome them for their own scheduled appointments. When you’re here for your own services, please arrange childcare so every guest can enjoy a relaxing visit. Salon & spa etiquette

Do you take walk-ins?

We’re appointment-based. Walk-ins and same-day appointments are welcome when we have room, but they can’t be guaranteed, so booking ahead is best. New guest guide

How can I pay, and should I tip?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, cash and Bellezza gift cards. Gratuity is always optional and appreciated; cash tips are welcome, and some providers also take tips by Venmo or Cash App.

What if I need to change my appointment?

Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice to change, reschedule or cancel an appointment. That gives us the chance to offer the time to another guest, and it respects your provider’s time. After a second no-show or same-day cancellation, a 50% deposit is required to schedule future services. Changes & cancellations policy
