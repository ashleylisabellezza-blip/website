Book online

Book hair, nails, facials, massage and more any time, on our online booking page (Meevo) or in the MeGo Client app.

Book an appointment Call 740-366-1604

Book online 24/7 on our online booking page (Meevo), in the MeGo Client app, or call us. Changes need 24 hours’ notice. Policies

What happens next

  1. Tap Book an appointment.
  2. Sign in, or create an account the first time. Meevo needs one to book.
  3. Choose your service, provider and time on our online booking page (Meevo).
  4. Need to change it later? Give us at least 24 hours’ notice.

Already know who you want? Every provider on Meet the Team has a Book with button that opens their own booking calendar.

Prices depend on your provider’s level (how levels work). Every price is on Services & prices. Keratin smoothing, platinum card color, fantasy color and specialty braiding need a consultation before you book.

Before you book

  • We ask for at least 24 hours’ notice for any change, reschedule or cancellation.
  • Cancelling or rescheduling within 24 hours counts as a same-day cancellation. After a no-show or same-day cancellation: first a reminder, then a 50% deposit for future bookings, then full prepayment.
  • Keratin treatments need a consultation and a $50 deposit.
  • Please silence your phone, keep voices low, and arrange childcare while you’re here for your own appointment.
