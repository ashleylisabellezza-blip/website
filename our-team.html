Ashley Basham

Owner & Master Hair Stylist
At Master level: women’s cut $60 · all-over color $90

Born and raised in Newark, Lisa and Ashley have created their dream. The two sisters, along with their brother, grew up in a family-owned business. We always knew one day we wanted to start one of our own. After going through cosmetology school the sisters have each worked apart in different salons. Until one day the opportunity to work together finally came. In 2008 when the economy was crashing Lisa and Ashley decided to purchase a house that was 1,800 sqft that they have transformed into a 5,500 sqft Salon and Spa. We opened Bellezza Salon and Day Spa in 2009 and have since done two additions. Our last addition was in 2022 where we also rebranded to Bellezza and Co adding a boutique to the mix. Lisa and Ashley are still currently behind the chair one day a week and the other time is spent growing the business.

  • Co-founded Bellezza with her sister Lisa in 2009
  • Behind the chair one day a week.
