Ashley Basham

Co-Founder, Co-Owner & Master Stylist

Ashley Basham began her beauty career after graduating from C-TEC (formerly Licking County Joint Vocational School) in 2005 and co-founded Bellezza & Co. with her sister, Lisa, in 2009. With over 20 years behind the chair, she has built her career around her clients, her team, and a genuine love for the industry.

Ashley specializes in razor cutting and short haircuts and is certified in hair extensions. She is beginning a transition away from spending the majority of her time behind the chair so she can be more involved in the broader business and continued growth of Bellezza.

  • C-TEC (formerly Licking County Joint Vocational School), 2005
  • Providing services since 2005
  • Ohio Advanced Cosmetologist license COSA.037201 (verify on Ohio eLicense)
  • Training: Hair extensions
  • Specialties: Razor cutting, Short haircuts, Hair extensions

Ashley’s services: Haircuts, color & extensions · Men’s cuts

Ask for Ashley when you book.

Lisa Jeffries

Co-Founder & Co-Owner

Lisa Jeffries began her beauty career after graduating from the American School of Hair Design in 2002 and co-founded Bellezza & Co. with her sister, Ashley, in 2009.
