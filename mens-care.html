Questions about booking

What if I need to change my appointment?

Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice to change, reschedule or cancel an appointment. That gives us the chance to offer the time to another guest, and it respects your provider’s time. To change an appointment, call us at 740-366-1604. Changes & cancellations policy

What happens if I miss an appointment?

A no-show or same-day cancellation means cancelling or rescheduling within 24 hours of your appointment. It may mean you lose any deposit on that appointment. The first time, a friendly reminder of our policy is noted in your account. The second time, a 50% deposit is required to schedule future services. The third time, full prepayment (100%) is required to book future appointments. No-show policy
