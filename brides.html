Thank you for choosing Bellezza & Co.

Thank you for choosing Bellezza & Co. to be a part of your special day! We are dedicated to making your wedding experience as beautiful and seamless as possible. Our talented team is here to help you create your dream look for both hair and makeup, ensuring you and your bridal party feel stunning on your big day.

To help us prepare for your celebration, please fill out the attached form. Once we receive your information, our Bridal Coordinator will reach out to you to discuss your vision, reserve your date, and address any questions you may have.

To enhance your experience, we recommend scheduling all services—except for formal up-dos, styling, and makeup—prior to the wedding day. This allows everyone to relax and fully enjoy the festivities.

Travel Information

If you’d like the convenience of having us come to you on your wedding day, we offer a travel team as an upgrade for your services! Please note that a travel fee will apply for all off-site services, and this fee will vary based on the size of your bridal party and the location of your event.

If you have any questions or need assistance, please don’t hesitate to reach out to our Bridal Coordinator at dsabo@bellezzaspaonline.com. We’re excited to help make your wedding day truly unforgettable!
