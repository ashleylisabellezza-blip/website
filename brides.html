Bridal questions

Can you come to our venue?

Yes. Our travel team is available as an upgrade for your services. A travel fee applies to all off-site services, and it varies with the size of your bridal party and the location of your event.

What should we book before the wedding day?

We recommend scheduling all services, except formal up-dos, styling and makeup, before the wedding day. That way everyone can relax and enjoy the festivities.

Who answers my inquiry?

Our Bridal Coordinator, by email. You can also write directly to dsabo@bellezzaspaonline.com.

What if our plans change?

We kindly request at least 24 hours’ notice for any appointment changes, reschedules, or cancellations. Read our full policies.
