Our story

Lisa and Ashley bought an 1,800 sq ft house in 2008.

Lisa Jeffries and Ashley Basham were born and raised in Newark and grew up in a family-owned business. After cosmetology school they each worked in other salons. They opened Bellezza in 2009 and have grown it to 5,500 sq ft, adding the boutique and the name Bellezza & Co. in 2022. Both are still behind the chair one day a week.
