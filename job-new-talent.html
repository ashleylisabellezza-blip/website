New Talent Stylist

Bellezza is always looking for New Talent to join our team. We work with local cosmetology schools with our internship program and training program for recently licensed cosmetologists. We customize the training program to fit the needs of each new team member in training, with the end goal being advanced technical skills and a strong knowledge of how to grow and maintain your clientele.

Formula for success includes

  • Ambitious — Able to self-start.
  • Vivacious — Lively and spirited. Naturally fun to be around and full of life.
  • Team player — You care about helping your team succeed, with no thought of what’s in it for you. When the team succeeds, you succeed.
  • Love to learn — Every day is a chance to learn something new, and education is the foundation of growth.
  • Goal setter — Identifying something that you want to accomplish and setting an action plan to accomplish it.

Team role

  • Weekly education classes include theory, mannequin and model work, and business mindset classes.
  • Assisting Senior and Master Stylists with their clients. This may include assisting with shampoos and toners, color application, styling, or general client experience.
  • Assist in the cleanliness of the salon.
  • Ability to work evenings and weekends.

This position requires a license from the Ohio State Cosmetology and Barber Board. If you are still in cosmetology school, you may apply for our Internship Program.
