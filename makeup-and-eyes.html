Brows

  • Eyebrow Shaping & Waxing$24 / 26

    Stray eyebrow hairs are removed to create a more defined and enhanced arch and brow shape.

  • Eyebrow Tinting$20 / 22

    Eyebrow tinting is the process of applying semi-permanent dye to enhance, shape, and define your brows.

  • HD Brows$40 / 44

    Eyebrow shape and wax with tint. High frequency is used to conceal redness.

  • Brows Lamination$55

    A smoothing and shaping treatment designed to create fuller, more defined brows. Brow lamination restructures the brow hairs to sit in a uniform direction, enhancing shape, symmetry, and density. Ideal for brows that are unruly, sparse, or uneven. Results last up to 6 weeks and can be customized for a soft, natural finish or a more bold, lifted look. Tinting and shaping may be added for a more polished result.

    Brow lamination is not recommended for clients using retinol or similar products.

  • Brows Lamination with Eyebrow Wax$79

    A smoothing and shaping treatment designed to create fuller, more defined brows. Brow lamination restructures hairs to sit in a uniform direction, enhancing shape, symmetry, and density. Ideal for unruly, sparse, or uneven brows. Results last up to 6 weeks and can be customized for a soft, natural or bold, lifted look. Includes brow wax and shaping.

    Not recommended for clients using retinol or similar products.

  • Bella Brows$90

    A complete brow transformation designed to create polished, balanced, and beautifully defined brows. This service includes brow lamination to smooth and set the brow hairs, a custom wax shaping to refine the brow line, and a tailored tint to enhance depth and definition. The result is fuller-looking, perfectly styled brows with a soft yet elevated finish that lasts for weeks.

Good to know

Before your brow, lash or makeup appointment

I use retinol. Can I have brow lamination?

We don’t recommend it. Brow lamination is not recommended for clients using retinol or similar products. Eyebrow shaping, waxing and tinting are listed on their own in the menu above.

Can I have a lash lift while pregnant?

We don’t recommend it. A lash lift, on its own or as part of Bella Eyes, is not recommended during pregnancy.

How do I get ready for a lash lift?

Please arrive to your appointment clean of any eye makeup.

What if I need to change my appointment?

We kindly request at least 24 hours’ notice for any appointment changes, reschedules, or cancellations. Call us at 740-366-1604. Read our full policies.
