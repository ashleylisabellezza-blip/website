    By level

    • Classic manicure$31–40

      This manicure consists of filing and shaping of the nails, cuticle work, hand massage, and the application of fingernail polish if desired.

      This manicure uses a soft gel in the form of polish. The gel polish acts as an overlay on the nail that lasts for at least two weeks.

      The dip powder nail technique offers added strength to your manicure.

    • Classic pedicure$48–56

      Classic pedicures include a warm foot soak, steam towel, light callus removal with a foot file, sugar scrub, shortening/shaping of the nails, cuticle work, a light foot massage, and nail polish (if desired).

      A luxury pedicure that targets dryness and calluses.
