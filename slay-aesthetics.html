Slay Aesthetics & Wellness at Bellezza

Botox, Fillers & Weight Loss

Shannon Francis, CNP, of Slay Aesthetics & Wellness, sees clients inside Bellezza & Co. on Mondays and Fridays, 9am–6pm. Aesthetic consultations are complimentary.

Botox® $12 per unit · Revanesse® Versa™ $549 per syringe · Microneedling from $325 · Compounded semaglutide $299 / month

Book with Shannon Call 740-366-1604

Slay appointments book through our online booking page (Meevo), or our front desk can book you. Bellezza gift cards are not accepted at Slay Aesthetics.

Your provider

Shannon Francis, CNP, Certified Nurse Practitioner

  • Master’s degree and BSN from The Ohio State University, plus a bachelor’s degree in Business from Ohio State.
  • About 15 years as a registered nurse, about 4 years as a nurse practitioner, and 4 years providing aesthetic and injectable services.
  • Advanced aesthetic training through Aesthetic Medical Educators Training (AMET), Merz Aesthetics, Allergan Aesthetics and RHA/Revance.
  • Practices under a standard care arrangement with her collaborating physician, Natalie Ferretti, MD, as Ohio requires for nurse practitioners.

Slay Aesthetics & Wellness is a separate business that operates inside Bellezza & Co. at 206 Deo Drive, Newark, on Mondays and Fridays, 9am–6pm. Its treatments are provided by Shannon, not by Bellezza’s salon and spa team.

Visit slay-aesthetics.com

Consultations

Aesthetic consultations are complimentary. Book one on its own, or have it at the start of your treatment appointment.

Hormone therapy (HRT for women, TRT for men) starts with an initial medical consultation and evaluation. Medical weight loss medication is chosen for each client individually.
